Privacy Policy

Your spending is personal. Here's what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated: October 2026

Data We Collect How We Use It Data Storage Third Parties Contact Us
01

Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run Spendly. This includes:

  • Account details you provide, such as your name and email address.
  • Expense entries you record, including amount, category, date, and description.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and login times, used to keep your account secure.
02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the service. We use it to:

  • Show your expenses, category breakdowns, and monthly summaries.
  • Authenticate you and protect your account from unauthorised access.
  • Send important account-related notices, such as policy updates.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are stored only in hashed form and are never visible to us. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and expense records are permanently removed.

04

Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted service providers, such as hosting providers, to operate the service. These providers only process data on our behalf and are required to keep it confidential. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

05

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or would like to access, correct, or delete your data, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.example. We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.
